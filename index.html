About Unique Touch

Every celebration, its own story.

Unique Touch Event Planner is a full-service event planning company based in Hartford, CT, specializing in creating memorable and personalized events for a variety of occasions.

With a keen attention to detail and a creative approach, Unique Touch works closely with clients to bring their vision to life. From weddings to corporate events, every event is made to feel unique and unforgettable.

Alicia Augustin, Unique Touch
